## BUDGET JUSTIFICATION

### Why This Budget is Reasonable

**Personnel (60%):** Industry standard for research grants is 50-70% on personnel. We're at 60%, appropriate for labor-intensive work (educational pilots, software development, community building).

**Research Activities (25%):** Direct research costs. Educational pilots require real resources (teacher training, materials, incentives). AI validation requires compute. Publications require fees.

**Infrastructure (10%):** Essential tools and travel. Conference presence is critical for paradigm adoption. Software tools enable efficient work.

**Community Building (5%):** Investment in sustainability. Strong documentation and training ensures framework outlives this grant.

**Administrative (5%):** Minimal overhead. Solo researcher means no institutional 30-50% overhead. Just essential accounting/legal.

---

### Cost-Effectiveness Comparison

**Traditional Academic Research Grant (Similar Scope):**
- University overhead: 30-50% → $77,000-$180,000 (this grant: $9,000 = 5%)
- Personnel: Typically 2-3 full-time RAs → $120,000+ (this grant: $108,000 = 1.5 FTEs)
- Equipment: Often $50,000+ (this grant: $3,000)
- Travel: Often $20,000+ (this grant: $9,000)
- **Typical total for comparable work: $300,000-$500,000**

**This Grant: $180,000 = 36-60% cost savings**

**Why?**
- No institutional overhead (solo researcher)
- New Zealand cost-of-living lower than US/UK
- Remote work reduces travel needs
- Open-source tools (free where possible)
- Lean startup approach (efficiency focus)
